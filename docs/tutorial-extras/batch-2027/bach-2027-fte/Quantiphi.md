# Quantiphi Interview Experience

**Article By**: Hari Kishore

**Company Name**: Quantiphi

**Job Description**: Machine Learning Engineer (MLE)

---

## Online Assessment Round

**Mode**: Online

**Duration**: 1 hour 30 minutes

**Content**:

* **No. of MCQ Questions**: 80 MCQs

  * 40 Aptitude
  * 40 Core CS
* **No. of DSA Questions**: 3

  * 2 Easy
  * 1 Medium-Hard

---

## Technical Round 1

**Mode**: Virtual

**Duration**: 20 minutes

### 1) Domain Questions

* Pick one of your projects and explain **all the technical aspects**.
* Explain the **complete RAG pipeline**.
* How will you implement a **retry mechanism on the Redis task queue**?
* What are the **trade-offs of using Kafka**?
* Questions on **RAG evaluation** and **RAGAS**.

### 2) DSA Questions

**Question 1:**

Find the **second largest element in an unsorted array** in **linear time**.

---

## Vibe Coding Round

**Duration**: 1 hour

### Task

* Build a solution based on the **given problem statement**.
* You can use **any AI tool** within the given time.
* This was a **non-elimination round**.

---

## Technical Round 2

**Mode**: Offline

**Duration**: 30 minutes

### 1) Domain Questions

* **RAG vs Fine-tuning** and their respective use cases.
* What is **`k` in KNN and K-means clustering**?
* Write code to implement **KNN in a 2D matrix**.
* Explain:

  * **Supervised vs. Unsupervised Learning**
  * **Overfitting vs. Underfitting**
* What is **hallucination**?
* How will you tackle hallucinations in an **RAG system**?
* What are **Guardrails**?
* Explain important **RAG evaluation metrics**.

---

## HR Round

**Mode**: Offline

**Duration**: 30 minutes

### 1) Questions

* Introduce yourself in **non-technical terms**.
* Tell me about your **family background**.
* **Guesstimation Problem**: Estimate the number of iPhones within a **5 km radius**.
* Answer **scenario-based questions**.
* Explain the **business model of Quantiphi**.
* What are the **products of Quantiphi**?

### 2) Advice

* **Confidence and communication skills matter the most in this round.**

---

## Final Note

* Prepare **RAG architecture and evaluation** thoroughly for an MLE role.
* Be comfortable explaining your projects from both a **technical and architectural perspective**.
* Revise important **Machine Learning fundamentals**, including supervised/unsupervised learning and overfitting/underfitting.
* Prepare **KNN and K-means clustering** concepts and be ready to implement them.
* Understand **RAG vs. Fine-tuning**, including when and why to use each approach.
* Prepare concepts around **hallucination, guardrails, and RAG evaluation metrics**.
* For system-design-oriented questions, understand **Redis queues, retry mechanisms, Kafka, scalability, and trade-offs**.
* Practice **guesstimation and scenario-based questions** for the HR round.
* Most importantly, maintain **confidence and clear communication** throughout the interview.

---

**All the best! 🚀**
