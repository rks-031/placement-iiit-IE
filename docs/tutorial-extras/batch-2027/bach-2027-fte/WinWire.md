# WinWire Interview Experience
**Article By:** Shubh Verma  

**Company Name:** WinWire

**Job Description:** Software Design Trainee

---

## Round 1 — Online Assessment

**Mode:** Online

**Duration:** 44 minutes

**Difficulty:** The questions were **very easy**.

**No. of Questions:** 60

**Breakdown:**

- **Aptitude:** 15 questions
- **Logical Reasoning:** 15 questions
- **Verbal Ability:** 15 questions
- **Core CS:** 15 questions
- **DSA:** 0 questions

---

## Round 2 — Technical Interview 1

### Topics Discussed

- **DSA Question**

  - 1 basic DSA question
  - **Difficulty:** Easy to very easy

- **DBMS & SQL**

  - 1 basic **SQL query**
  - **Difficulty:** Easy to very easy

- **Project Discussion**

  There was an **in-depth discussion** on both my internship project and personal project.

  My personal project was an **Agentic AI project**, and the interviewer was particularly interested in it.

  Questions focused on:

  - Project implementation
  - How the project works
  - Technical architecture
  - Implementation details of the Agentic AI system

- **Domain & Technology Stack**

  - Questions related to my **project's domain**
  - Questions related to the **technology stack** used in my projects

- **Internship Experience**

  A situational question was asked:

  - **What would you do if you found out that you had made a mistake in a team project?**

- **Other Questions**

  - Why did you choose the **CSE branch**?

---

## Round 3 — Technical Interview 2

### Topics Discussed

- **DSA Question**

  - 1 basic DSA question
  - **Difficulty:** Easy to very easy

- **Python Fundamentals**

  Questions were asked on **Python fundamentals**.

- **Project Discussion**

  An **end-to-end explanation** of both my internship project and personal project was discussed.

  My personal project was an **Agentic AI project**.

  Questions included:

  - Explain your project end-to-end.
  - How does the Agentic AI project work?
  - How would you **design and build agentic workflows**?
  - How would you approach the implementation of an agentic system?

- **Internship Experience**

  - How would you **improvise or upgrade your internship project** if you were given more than 2 months?

---

## Round 4 — HR Round

### Topics Discussed

- **Introduction**

  - Introduce yourself.

- **Behavioural Questions**

  - How would you handle a **disagreement with a teammate**?
  - Where do you see yourself in **5 years**?

- **Company Discussion**

  - **Why WinWire?**

- **Other Questions**

  - Workplace **scenario-based questions**
  - Are you **willing to relocate**?

---

## Round 5 — Group Discussion

### Details

After the Online Assessment, an **AI interview round** was initially scheduled.

However, due to a **technical glitch**, the AI interview round was replaced with a **Group Discussion (GD)**.

Some of the GD topics included:

- **Work-Life Balance vs. Salary**
- **Pre-planning vs. Being Spontaneous**
- **Gen Z vs. Millennials**

---

## Final Note

- Prepare **basic DSA and SQL** thoroughly, as the coding questions were at an easy to very easy level.
- Be ready to explain your **projects end-to-end**, especially your individual contribution.
- If you have an **Agentic AI project**, understand its architecture, implementation, and workflow deeply.
- Prepare basic **Python fundamentals**.
- Practice **situational and behavioural questions**, particularly around teamwork and handling mistakes.
- Be prepared for **GD topics** involving workplace, generational, and lifestyle-related issues.
- During GD, focus on **clear communication, relevant points, active listening, and not interrupting others**.
- Prepare a strong answer for **"Why WinWire?"**.
- Be prepared to explain your career goals and where you see yourself in the next **5 years**.
