# TCS Interview Experience

**Company Name**: TCS

**Job Description**: Systems Engineer

---

## Online Assessment Round

**Mode**: Online at Centers

**Duration**: As per the TCS NQT assessment pattern.

The overall assessment consisted of:

* Aptitude
* Reasoning
* Verbal Ability
* Advanced Quantitative & Reasoning
* Coding / Programming

### Foundation Round

| Section                   | Questions |   Duration |
| ------------------------- | --------: | ---------: |
| Numerical Ability (Quant) |        20 | 25 minutes |
| Verbal Ability (English)  |        25 | 25 minutes |
| Reasoning Ability         |        20 | 25 minutes |

### Advanced Round

| Section                           | Questions |   Duration |
| --------------------------------- | --------: | ---------: |
| Advanced Quantitative & Reasoning |        14 | 25 minutes |
| Coding / Programming              |         2 | 90 minutes |

---

## Technical Round

The round started with a **formal self-introduction**.

I was then asked about my **previous internship experience** and the work I had done during my internship.

### 1) Programming Questions

Questions were asked about the programming languages mentioned in my resume.

**Questions included:**

* What programming languages are you comfortable with?
* What is the **difference between C and C++**?
* Questions related to **Java** and **Python**.
* Having knowledge of multiple programming languages was considered an advantage.

---

### 2) DSA / Coding Questions

The following coding questions were asked:

1. Sort all the elements of an **array alphabetically**.
2. **Reverse a string**.
3. Write code for **file operations**, including:

   * Reading files
   * Writing files
   * Performing operations on files

---

### 3) SQL / DBMS Questions

Questions included:

* What is the difference between different types of **SQL JOINs**?
* What is **DDL**?
* What is **DML**?
* Give examples of DDL and DML commands.

---

### 4) Questions on Technologies & Projects

Questions were asked about the **new technologies mentioned in my resume**, including:

* AI / ML
* Cloud technologies
* Other technical projects

I was asked to:

* Explain the technologies used in my projects.
* Explain how those technologies were implemented.
* Discuss the technical aspects of my projects.

Some **CS fundamentals** were also discussed.

---

### 5) Technical + Managerial Questions

Since this was a **Technical + Managerial (TR + MR)** round, some questions were designed to evaluate:

* Patience
* Confidence
* Reasoning ability
* Problem-solving approach

For example:

* How many interviews have you appeared for so far?
* Some reasoning-based questions were also asked.

---

## HR Round

The HR round started with a **formal introduction**.

### Questions Asked

* What are your **strengths and weaknesses**?
* Are you comfortable working **day/night shifts**?
* Are you comfortable with **work flexibility**?
* What are your **hobbies and interests**?
* Who is the **CEO of TCS**?
* What do you know about **TCS**?
* What are the products and services offered by TCS?

**Duration**: Approximately 5 minutes

The HR round was relatively **short**.

---

## Final Note

* Be thoroughly prepared with **everything mentioned on your resume**.
* Revise programming fundamentals and be comfortable with the languages listed on your resume.
* Prepare basic **DSA and coding problems**, especially strings, arrays, and file handling.
* Revise **SQL and DBMS fundamentals**, particularly JOINs, DDL, and DML.
* Be prepared to explain your **projects and the technologies used in them**.
* Revise basic **AI/ML and cloud concepts** if they are mentioned on your resume.
* For the Technical + Managerial round, focus on **confidence, patience, communication, and reasoning ability**.
* Before the HR round, know basic information about **TCS, its leadership, products, and services**.

**All the very best for your TCS interview and your future career! 🚀**

**Stay confident, be prepared with everything mentioned in your resume, and give your best. Wishing you success and a bright future ahead!**
