## Round 2 — Technical Interview

### Topics Discussed

- **Projects & Resume Discussion**

  A detailed discussion was conducted on the projects mentioned in the resume. Candidates were expected to explain their projects, implementation and individual contributions.

- **DSA**

  Easy to Medium-level **DSA questions** were asked during the interview.

- **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)**

  Questions were asked on fundamental **OOP concepts** and their practical understanding.

- **Operating Systems (OS)**

  Basic to intermediate-level questions related to **Operating Systems** were discussed.

- **Computer Networking**

  Candidates were asked questions covering the **basics of Computer Networks**.

- **DBMS & SQL**

  Some candidates were asked **SQL-related questions** from DBMS.

- **System Design**

  Very basic **System Design concepts** were discussed to evaluate the candidate's understanding of software architecture and design fundamentals.

---

## Key Topics to Prepare

- **DSA:** Easy to Medium-level problems
- **OOP:** Core OOP concepts and practical examples
- **Operating Systems:** Fundamental OS concepts
- **Computer Networks:** Networking basics
- **DBMS:** SQL and fundamental database concepts
- **System Design:** Basic architecture and design concepts
- **Projects:** Complete understanding of every project mentioned in the resume

---

## Pro Tips

- Prepare **DSA fundamentals** thoroughly, especially Easy to Medium-level problems.
- Be ready to explain every project mentioned on your **resume in detail**.
- Clearly understand your **individual contribution** to each project.
- Revise core **OOP concepts** with practical examples.
- Prepare fundamental concepts of **Operating Systems and Computer Networks**.
- Practice **SQL queries** and revise important DBMS concepts.
- Have a basic understanding of **System Design**, including components, architecture and scalability.
- Most importantly, **know everything mentioned on your resume**.
